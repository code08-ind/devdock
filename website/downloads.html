Download DevDock

Free for macOS and Windows. No account, no telemetry. Click a download and it starts straight away; the steps to open DevDock the first time come up next to it.

Get the installers from the releases page on GitHub.

Needs macOS 12 Monterey or later (Apple silicon or Intel), or Windows 10 or 11 (64-bit). DevDock isn’t code-signed yet; the steps after downloading show how to open it the first time. Once installed, DevDock checks once a day for a newer version and tells you.
